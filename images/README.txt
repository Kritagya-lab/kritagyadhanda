This folder holds your images.

------------------------------------------------------------
FULL-PAGE BACKGROUND PHOTO  (the one that covers the website)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Save your photo in THIS folder and name it exactly:
       background.jpg
   (The mountain photo you shared — just drag it here and rename it.)
2. That's it — js/data.js already points to "images/background.jpg".
   Refresh the website and the photo will cover the whole page,
   with your content sitting on a soft frosted-glass panel.

- Want a different file name (e.g. PNG)? Open js/data.js and change
  the "background" value to match, e.g. "images/background.png".
- To turn the background OFF, set  background: ""  in js/data.js.
- Large photos are fine, but ~1600px wide keeps the site fast.

------------------------------------------------------------
HEADSHOT  (optional, round photo in the header)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Save a square photo here, e.g. "headshot.jpg".
2. Open js/data.js and set:  photo: "images/headshot.jpg"
   Leave photo as "" to show your initials in a circle instead.

You can delete this README.txt once your images are added.
